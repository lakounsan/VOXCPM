Studio0808 VoxCPM AI Speech Workstation

An offline desktop AI speech workstation powered by OpenBMB VoxCPM2, delivering ultra-realistic voice cloning and expressive speech generation.

🚀 Quick Start & Model Deployment

📦 Workstation Package (Portable Windows Release)

Unzip and run directly on Windows without configuring Python. Model weights can be downloaded with 1-click inside the app Settings tab.

📥 Cloud Download

This tool runs 100% offline. On first use, download the model weights (~4.6GB). Follow these simple steps:

1

Download & Verify Model in Settings

Go to the Settings tab. Select a mirror (Hugging Face Official, Mirror, or ModelScope), then click "Start Download / Check Model". The downloader supports HTTP Range resume and auto-retry.

2

Choose Speech Generation Mode

Choose Voice Design (no reference audio), Voice Clone (3-10s reference audio), or Ultimate Clone (reference audio + transcript).

3

Synthesize & Play Audio

Type your text, click the pink "🚀 Start Speech Synthesis" button. Once generated, click the green "▶ Play Audio" button to listen!

🎤 Live Voice Recording Studio

Record your voice directly on your PC to immediately test voice cloning features.

  • Microphone Detection: Select any system microphone with real-time refresh.
  • Pink Ripple Waveform: Real-time visual feedback confirms your mic is capturing sound.
  • One-Click Apply: Directly applies your recording to Voice Clone or Ultimate Clone with prompt text.

✨ Voice Design (Create from Scratch)

Generate unique character voices without reference audio by describing vocal qualities in parentheses:

(A gentle young female voice, smiling) Hello and welcome to VoxCPM!
(An old man, deep American accent, calm) It is quiet tonight.
(A young woman, Cambodian accent, gentle and sweet voice) សួស្តី! នេះគឺជាសំឡេងដែលបង្កើតឡើងដោយ AI។

Batch Synthesis (Audiobook Mode)

Paste text line-by-line (one sentence per line). Click "📋 Batch Synthesis" to sequentially generate every sentence into separate high-quality WAV files.

👥 Voice Clone (Fast Replication)

Replicates tone, emotion, and timbre using only 3 to 10 seconds of clean reference voice. Ideal length: 5–8 seconds without background music.

👑 Ultimate Clone (Seamless Continuation)

Provide both reference audio and its exact spoken transcript. The model seamlessly continues speaking your target text with 100% emotional continuity, natural breathing, and identical acoustic timbre.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What languages are supported?

VoxCPM2 supports over 30 languages including English, Khmer, Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, and German.

Q: How to switch interface languages?

Use the language dropdown at the bottom of the left sidebar to toggle between English, ភាសាខ្មែរ (Khmer), and 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese) anytime!
